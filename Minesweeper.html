Flags
Flags mark suspected mines and protect cells from accidental reveals. Flag count is limited to the mine count. Remove by flagging the same cell again.
How to Win
Reveal every non-mine cell. Numbers show adjacent mine count. First click is always safe.
3BV & Efficiency
3BV is the minimum clicks needed to solve a board. Efficiency = your clicks ÷ 3BV. Lower is better; 1.00 is perfect.
