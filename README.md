A Minesweeper game with a wide variety of modes ranging from a classic mode with varying difficulties and custom boards, a full roguelike mode with 26+ perks, 10 curses, 10 boss boards, permanent unlocks, and loadouts, and smaller modes such as an Endurance, Speed Run, No-Flag, and Practice modes. The game includes 55 levels of progression, 62 achievements, 15 earnable titles, daily challenges with streak tracking, a replay viewer with full playback controls, a statistics dashboard with click heatmaps and win-rate charts, cosmetic customization.
